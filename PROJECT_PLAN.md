## Key Themes & Continuity

### Recurring Concepts
- **Sponge vs. Seasoning** — The central inversion that reframes your spiritual stance
- **Three Dimensions of Practice** — Mind (daily), Home (monthly), Life (continuous)
- **Preservation through Boundaries** — Staying distinct to function as salt
- **Presence as Active Force** — Not passive absorption but engaged transformation

### Biblical Anchors
- Matthew 5:13 — "You are the salt of the earth"
- Colossians 4:6 — "Seasoned with salt"
- Numbers 18:19 & 2 Chronicles 13:5 — "Covenant of salt"
- Leviticus 2:13 — "Covenant of salt" in offerings
- Mark 9:49-50 — "Salted with fire"

### Practical Progressions
**Part III** builds progressively from manageable (mind) to complex (life):
1. Daily practices first (easier to implement)
2. Monthly rituals second (moderate commitment)
3. Life-long integration third (ongoing intention)

## Writing Standards

See WRITING_GUIDELINES.md for detailed standards. Summary:

- **Voice:** Warm, accessible, theologically grounded
- **Structure:** Opening image → Development → Practical application → Closing
- **Length:** 6,000-9,000 words per chapter
- **Specificity:** Every claim grounded in Scripture or experience
- **Practice:** 2-3 concrete practices per chapter
- **Tone shifts:** Foundation (scholarly), Dynamics (philosophical), Practice (actionable), Deeper Work (contemplative)

## Notes & Considerations

- **Accessibility:** Keep theological references clear but not simplified; readers can grow into deeper understanding
- **Inclusivity:** Acknowledging multiple Christian traditions while maintaining biblical grounding
- **Practicality:** Every practice must be genuinely helpful, not performative
- **Honesty:** Acknowledge where practices might not work; don't oversell
- **Integration:** Show how all three practices (mind, home, life) work together, not in isolation
